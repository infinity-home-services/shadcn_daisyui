Sign-in page

One sign-in page for every app that signs people in with Microsoft. It shows the application's name, Sign in with Microsoft, and what happened last. Development builds add a development sign-in. Phones get the form edge to edge, tablets a card, desktops a picture panel beside the form.

Desktop

From 64rem. The left half names the application over the app's picture (or the primary colour in light, a card in dark, without one). The form is on the right.

Tablet and phone

Tablets (40rem to 64rem) get the form in a card, over the picture when there is one. Phones (below 40rem) get it edge to edge. The button is 44px tall wherever touch is likely.

States

What happened last shows above the button. A refusal or a failure is red, anything else a plain notice. Pick a size to see every state at it.

Usage

The page is the whole screen, so the sign-in template renders it on its own, outside Layouts.app/1, with the flash group beside it. The app passes its own name, picture and routes.

heex 
<.sign_in_page
  app_name="Atlas"
  org="Acme Inc."
  tagline="Sales and service reporting for every Acme store."
  image={~p"/images/sign-in.jpg"}
  href={~p"/auth/entra"}
  configured={@entra_configured}
  notice={Phoenix.Flash.get(@flash, :sign_in)}
>
  <:development :if={@development?} label="Sign in as a role">
    <.form for={@form} action={~p"/sign-in/development"}>…</.form>
  </:development>
  <:footer><.link href={~p"/privacy"}>Privacy</.link></:footer>
</.sign_in_page>
<.flash_group flash={@flash} />

A controller that refuses a sign-in, or signs someone out, puts the notice's key in the flash under :sign_in and redirects to the sign-in page. The page words it, so every app says it the same way.

elixir 
defp refuse(conn, reason) do
  conn |> put_flash(:sign_in, notice(reason)) |> redirect(to: ~p"/sign-in")
end

defp notice(reason) when reason in [:not_set_up, :no_app_role, :no_brand], do: "no_access"
defp notice(:removed), do: "ended"
defp notice(:entra_unavailable), do: "unavailable"
defp notice(:canceled), do: "canceled"
defp notice(:not_configured), do: "not_configured"
defp notice(_bad_state_or_reply), do: "failed"

def delete(conn, _params) do
  conn |> Auth.sign_out() |> put_flash(:sign_in, "signed_out") |> redirect(to: ~p"/sign-in")
end

Notices

Key Says
signed_out You're signed out. Microsoft asks which account to use next time.
unavailable Microsoft can't be reached right now.
canceled Microsoft didn't complete the sign-in.
failed The sign-in didn't complete.
no_access You don't have access to Atlas yet. Your manager gives access.
ended Your access to Atlas has ended.
not_configured Microsoft sign-in isn't set up on this server.

configured={false} shows the not-set-up notice and disables the button. A key the page doesn't know shows nothing. Anything else is an <:alert title="…"> of the app's own.

Development sign-in

The <:development> slot sits under a divider. The app renders it only in development and test builds, as it does the route behind it. Each entry with a label is a section that folds away, and the whole development sign-in folds away too. The browser remembers what's hidden, so a developer can keep the page as production shows it.

The app's look

The package stays neutral, and the app brings its look. image fills the desktop panel and the page behind the tablet card, darkened so the text on it reads. The colours come from the theme: a brand theme's --sign-in-accent runs through the application's name over the picture, and its --app-icon (or its primary) colours the app's icon. Sign in with Microsoft stays neutral in every brand: black in light, white in dark. A brand scope (data-brand) brands just the sign-in page in an app that runs the neutral theme. <:panel> replaces the panel's centre, and <:logo> the icon tile over the form.

css 
/* the brand theme's CSS: the whole app, or just the sign-in page */
[data-theme="acme"], [data-theme="acme-dark"] { --sign-in-accent: var(--primary); }
[data-brand="acme"] {
  --primary: oklch(0.62 0.19 260);
  --primary-foreground: oklch(0.985 0 0);
  --color-primary: var(--primary);
  --color-primary-content: var(--primary-foreground);
  --sign-in-accent: var(--primary);
}
heex 
<.sign_in_page app_name="Atlas" image={~p"/images/sign-in.jpg"} data-brand="acme" …>
  <:panel>
    <p class="sign-in-app-name">Atlas</p>
    <span class="sign-in-rule"></span>
    <p class="sign-in-tagline">Reporting for every store<br /><strong>in one place</strong></p>
  </:panel>
</.sign_in_page>
